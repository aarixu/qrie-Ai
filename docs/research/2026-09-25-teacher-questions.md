# Kal teacher/school se kya poochhna hai

Ek hi cheez maapni hai: **kya ye insaan roz app kholega aur data daalega?**
Pichhli baar `f` kisi ne naapa nahi tha aur product band ho gaya. Is baar wo
an-naapa number yahi hai.

Sunna zyada, batana kam. Product mat bech — abhi sirf pata karna hai.

---

## Pehle unki aaj ki zindagi (product ka zikr bhi mat karo)

1. **Aaj homework kaise dete ho?** Blackboard pe likhwate ho, ya WhatsApp pe
   photo bhejte ho, ya kuch aur?
2. **Kaun sa software already use karte ho?** School ka koi ERP hai —
   Teachmint, Entab, CampusCare, kuch bhi? Usme student list, sections,
   timetable already padi hai?
   > *Kyun:* agar unka ERP pehle se roster rakhta hai, toh "sections" hamari
   > table hai ya unki copy — ye poora design badal deta hai.
3. **Copy kab check karte ho, aur kitni der lagti hai?** School mein, ghar pe,
   kitne bachche?
4. **Bachche ka kaam jama na ho toh pata kaise chalta hai?**

## Phir wo jagah jahan dard hai

5. **Din ka sabse chidchida kaam kaun sa hai?** Bas chup ho jao aur sunn lo.
6. **Pichhli baar koi school app/software try kiya tha? Kya hua?**
   > *Kyun:* "chhod diya kyunki..." — uska jawab hamara sabse bada sabak hai.

## Ab hamara plan — par sawaal ki tarah, dawa ki tarah nahi

7. **"Agar main homework app pe daal doon aur bachche app pe jama karein — tum
   roz khologe?"** Haan bole toh **"roz kitna time de paoge?"** poochho.
   Agar wo 2 minute se zyada bolein, to wo ya toh jhooth hai ya wo bahut
   mehnti hain — dono jaanna zaroori hai.
   > **Ye list ka sabse zaroori sawaal hai.**
8. **"Bachche notebook pe likh kar photo daal dein — chalega, ya tum chahte ho
   ki wo app pe hi type karein?"**
   > *Kyun:* teeno answer types ka order isi se tay hoga.
9. **"MCQ hum apne aap check kar dein, baaki tum check karo — theek hai?"**
   > *Kyun:* agar wo chahti hain ki AI sab check kare, to plan badalta hai aur
   > kharcha aata hai.
10. **"Tumhare bachchon ke paas apna phone hai?"** Kitne %? Ghar pe net?
    > *Kyun:* "apna phone" wala jawab humne maan liya hai. Agar galat hai to
    > shared-device ka poora rasta banana padega.

## Aakhir mein — ye poochhne se mat darna

11. **"Ye kaam ka lage to school kitna dega — per bachcha per saal, ya ek
    saath?"** Aur: **"kharidne ka faisla kaun karta hai — tum, principal, ya
    trust?"**
12. **"Agar main 2 hafte mein ek chhota version banaun, tum apni ek class pe
    try karogi?"**
    > Agar wo "haan" bolein — wo hamari pehli pilot hain. Unka board, unki
    > classes, unke subjects. Hum unke hisaab se banayenge, guess ke hisaab
    > se nahi.

---

## Ek cheez jo maang kar aana

**Unki student list ki ek Excel** (ek section ki bhi chalegi) —
ya sirf ek screenshot. Column ke naam dekhne hain: roll number kaisa dikhta
hai, naam ka format kya hai, section kaise likha hai.

Pehle 10 school hum haath se import karenge, toh unki asli file ka shape
dekhna sabse kaam ki cheez hai jo kal mil sakti hai.

---

## Jo NAHI karna

- AI tutor ka zikr mat karo. Wo baad mein aayega. Abhi wo bataoge to poori
  baat usi pe chali jaayegi aur tum homework wala sach nahi sunn paoge.
- "White-label" mat bolo. Bolo: *"app pe tumhare school ka naam aur logo
  dikhega."*
- Agar wo kahein "haan bahut achha hai" — wo **jawab nahi hai**. Poochho
  *"kal subah kya alag karogi?"*
