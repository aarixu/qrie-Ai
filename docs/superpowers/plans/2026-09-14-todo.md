## Phase 3, broken out

Ordered by what unblocks what.

**3.1 — `ConceptState`, and retiring the bare float.** `Mastery =
Record<string, number>` cannot hold a date, which is why `decayMastery` has
been correct and uncallable for weeks and why spaced retrieval is structurally
impossible rather than merely unbuilt. Fields per decision 12: mastery,
unassistedMastery, assistedPerformance, observations, lastPracticedAt,
lastUnassistedSuccessAt, memoryStrength, nextReviewAt, retrievalCount,
retrievalAccuracy. `nextReviewAt` and `MisconceptionState` are in the schema
from day one and NOT driven — so turning them on later is wiring, not a
migration.

**3.2 — The assisted/unassisted split.** The ledger now records the rung that
was actually in force, so this is arithmetic over rows that already exist. Rung
0 is the only one that may move `unassistedMastery`.

**3.3 — `concept_state` server-side.** A table keyed `(student_id,
outcome_id)`, own-rows RLS, `org_id` from day one like every other table. The
device keeps a replica; the ledger stays the source of truth.

**3.4 — Rewire every writer.** `stores/outcomes.ts`'s counters, the diagnostic,
the quiz. Nothing writes belief directly after this.

**3.5 — Rebuild from the ledger, byte for byte.** The check the plan already
demands: a session replayed from its events must reproduce the model exactly. A
ledger that cannot rebuild the model is a log, not a record.

**3.6 — Elo, per student and per probe.** From the research, and the one
component that gets strictly better as students arrive: no training corpus, no
fitting step, ~50 lines. It replaces `params.ts`'s empty `items: {}`, which
will otherwise stay empty for a year. Fitting slip/guess per probe on day-one
data is the 0.46 defect in a new costume — the file already says these are
"priors we are prepared to be wrong about", and that should be obeyed.

**3.7 — Three pure functions over the ledger.** Assistance score per outcome
(ASSISTments found it predicted external tests better than percent-correct),
hint abuse, wheel-spinning. Zero model cost. Wheel-spinning is only computable
on the quiz and placement surfaces at four asks a class — build it there and
say so.

**Not in Phase 3, deliberately:** no DKT, SAKT, DKVMN or fine-tuned LLM tracer.
Logistic methods beat them except on the largest datasets, and their output
cannot be shown to a student — which is fatal for a product whose pitch is
showing them what it found.

## After the MVP

Phases 5–9 are unchanged and out of scope: misconception diagnosis, spaced
retrieval and the controller, the learning scientist, the soft models, and the
Play Store / LMS. Their fields exist in the Phase 3 schema so switching them on
is wiring.

One thing worth carrying forward from the research: the misconception bank has
13,482 tagged distractors and **13,380 distinct labels** — a reuse ratio of
1.01, so no misconception can ever accumulate evidence across items. Collapsing
that taxonomy per subject-and-grade is Phase 5's real first task, and it is a
weekend with an embedding model rather than new content.
