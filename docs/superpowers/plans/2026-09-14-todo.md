**4.8 in full, because that row is a decision about money.** One class was
measured on 2026-09-13 — `tools/measure/last-class.json`, priced by
`tools/measure/model-cost.mjs`. Preparing it cold took ~96 seconds (95,974 ms):
23s planning, 42s drawing, and the other ~31s checking the plan and then
repairing it, because that run's first plan failed validation — `beats[17]` was
both taught and checked in the same beat — and the repair is a second planner
call made after the planning clock has already stopped. A topic whose plan
passes first time does not pay those 31 seconds, so ~96s is what one measured
run took, not a floor.

The ₹18.98 is prep ₹9.08 plus a cached class ₹9.90. Only the ₹9.08 is paid once
per topic, so ₹9.08 is the price of warming one topic **if warming means
preparing it**. It is not the price if warming also means recording the audio
once: the same script prices the whole catalogue at ₹17,831.74 for 1,099 topics
— ₹9,977.13 of prep plus ₹7,854.61 of narration and replies — which is ₹16.23 a
topic, ₹7.15 more than prep alone. Which of those two he is buying is the
decision the row is asking for.

The cold figure falls to ₹10.48 (₹9.08 prep + ₹1.40 playback) only once the
voice is self-hosted, and that needs a Piper voice picked and a VPS — the first
two rows of this table, neither of them done. The row read ~50s and ~₹14 until
2026-09-14: the 50s was `class-prep`'s pre-measurement README figure, and
nothing in the tree produces ₹14.
