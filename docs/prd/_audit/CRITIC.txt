SUMMARY
I read all six PRDs (docs/prd/0 through 6, 5,487 lines) and then went to the source for anything I suspected was uncovered. The documents are unusually strong on engineering risk — spend caps, tenancy, the fold defect, the cost ladder — and they share a blind spot: they are written by and for the person building the software, so almost everything that is a *decision about a business, a school day or an Indian regulation* rather than a file has no owner. Seventeen gaps below, strongest first. The four I would act on this week: (1) PRD 3's roster credential is derived from a single never-rotatable secret while the ids are served by an unauthenticated route, so one env var is the security of 500 children's accounts; (2) there is no way for a teacher or a school admin to exist or sign in at all — `staff_invite` is a table with no endpoint, nothing creates the school org, and the teacher `app_metadata` that `my_student_ids()` depends on is written by nothing, which means MVP-2's demo has no teacher; (3) the Piper VPS, the lever the whole Rs 5 argument rests on, is sized by monthly price and never by concurrency — one 4 vCPU box measured at 10.8x realtime single-stream against a school needing roughly 17-25x sustained, with the designed fallback being the vendor, so the cost spikes exactly at peak; (4) a child who discloses abuse or self-harm is detected well by packages/safety/src/screen.ts and then reaches nobody — the flag is `neverSync` localStorage, PRD 5's only reader is "hand the phone to a grown-up", and POCSO, self-harm, helpline and mandatory reporting appear zero times across all six documents. Also entirely absent, each verified by grep over the PRD set: GST/entity/tax-invoice/TDS and how a school actually pays; refunds, chargebacks and mandate revocation; any dispute or correction path for the immutable progress card (which `unique (user_id, kind, period_end)` actively forecloses); breach notification and a data-processing agreement; TRAI DLT registration for the OTP that the entire funnel starts with; data export and what a school or a student takes when they leave; which exam board school #1 teaches (the syllabus JSON has no board field, while the enrol form collects one); teacher training and a school onboarding operation; support load and who answers it; a safety screen on generated images; contest legality and prize tax; a conformance target and a colour-vision check on the new palette; and a continuity story for a one-person supplier a school is about to depend on.
